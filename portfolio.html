Employee Attrition & Retention Analysis

An end-to-end HR analytics project using PostgreSQL and Power BI to identify where employee turnover is highest across job roles, tenure groups, and employee segments.

1,470 Employee Records 3 Business Questions SQL + Power BI

Business Questions

  • Which departments and job roles have the highest attrition rates?
  • At what stage of employee tenure is attrition highest?
  • Which employee segments have the highest attrition rates?

Key Insights

  • Sales Representatives had the highest department and job-role attrition rate at approximately 39.8%.
  • Employees with 0–2 years at the company had the highest tenure-based attrition rate at approximately 29.8%.
  • Employees working overtime with low job satisfaction had the highest employee-segment attrition rate at approximately 36.6%.

Skills & Techniques Used

  • PostgreSQL
  • SQL
  • CTEs
  • Power BI
  • Data Storytelling

Business Value

The analysis helps HR identify employee groups with higher turnover and highlights areas worth investigating further, including overtime, job satisfaction, early-tenure support, compensation, and career growth.

Housing Affordability Analysis - Peoria, Arizona

A Power BI analysis examining whether household income kept pace with rising home values in Peoria, Arizona.

Business Question

Are homes becoming less affordable in Peoria because median home values are increasing faster than median household income?

Key Insights

  • Median home values increased faster than household income.
  • The affordability ratio shows homes became more expensive relative to local income.
  • The dashboard highlights the widening gap between income and home prices.

Skills & Techniques Used

  • Power BI
  • Power Query
  • DAX
  • Data Modeling
  • Regression Analysis

Business Value

This analysis helps users understand housing affordability trends and supports discussions around housing pressure, income growth, and long-term financial planning.

Crime in Los Angeles Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Los Angeles crime data from 2020 to 2024 across time, location, crime type, and other incident characteristics.

Business Question

When and where are crimes happening most often, and what patterns can be found by time of day, crime type, and location?

Key Insights

  • Time-of-day analysis helps identify when crime activity is highest.
  • Geographic analysis highlights areas with higher concentrations of reported incidents.
  • Crime-type and weapon breakdowns provide more context behind reported incidents.

Skills & Techniques Used

  • Power BI
  • Data Cleaning
  • Geospatial Analysis
  • Time Analysis
  • Interactive Reporting

Business Value

The dashboard helps users identify patterns by location, time, and incident type to support public-safety reporting, resource planning, and clearer communication of crime trends.
