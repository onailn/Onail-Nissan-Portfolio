Employee Attrition & Retention Analysis

An end-to-end HR analytics project using PostgreSQL and Power BI to identify where employee turnover is highest across job roles, tenure groups, and employee segments.

1,470 Employee Records 3 Business Questions SQL + Power BI

Business Questions

  • Which departments and job roles have the highest attrition rates?
  • At what stage of employee tenure is attrition highest?
  • Which employee segments have the highest attrition rates?

Key Insights

  • Sales Representatives had the highest department and job-role attrition rate at approximately 39.8%.
  • Employees with 0–2 years at the company had the highest tenure-based attrition rate at approximately 29.8%.
  • Employees working overtime with low job satisfaction had the highest segment attrition rate at approximately 36.6%.

Skills & Techniques Used

  • PostgreSQL
  • SQL
  • CTEs
  • Power BI
  • Data Storytelling

Business Value

The analysis helps HR identify employee groups with higher turnover and prioritize further investigation around overtime, satisfaction, early-tenure support, compensation, and career growth.
